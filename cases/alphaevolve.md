# More usable capacity in Google's data centers

**Announced:** 2025-05-14 · **Category:** Infrastructure · **System:** AlphaEvolve

**Evidence:** Provider-reported deployment · **Sources checked:** 2026-09-17

## What happened

Google reported that a scheduling heuristic discovered using AlphaEvolve recovered, on average, 0.7% of its worldwide compute resources. Its May 2025 announcement described the solution as already deployed for over a year. [1]

## What AI did

Gemini models proposed code, and AlphaEvolve used automated evaluation and evolutionary search to improve candidate algorithms. The scheduling result concerned Google's Borg infrastructure. [1]

## What humans did

Engineers supplied the problem, evaluation process, and operational environment, then integrated the result into production. [1]

## Evidence

1. [AlphaEvolve technical account](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphaevolve-a-gemini-powered-coding-agent-for-designing-advanced-algorithms/): see the data center scheduling section; a technical report is linked there.

## Limits

The 0.7% figure is Google's report from its own fleet, not an independent audit or a result measured by this repository. It describes recovered compute capacity, not an equivalent percentage reduction in electricity bills, emissions, or spending.

This entry records the 2025 announcement, not a claim about the current performance of every deployment.
