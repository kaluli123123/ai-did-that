# Over 200 million protein structure predictions

**Released:** 2022-07-28 · **Category:** Biology · **System:** AlphaFold

**Evidence:** Project-reported result · **Sources checked:** 2026-09-17

## What happened

DeepMind and EMBL-EBI expanded the AlphaFold Protein Structure Database to over 200 million predictions and made them freely accessible for research. This entry records the 2022 release milestone. [1, 2]

## What AI did

AlphaFold predicted three-dimensional protein structures from biological sequence information. The collection offers researchers computational starting points for investigating proteins. [1]

## What humans did

Researchers developed and evaluated the model using biological data and prior experimental work. DeepMind and EMBL-EBI built and published the database. Scientists assess the predictions for their particular research questions. [1, 2]

## Evidence

1. [AlphaFold project and release timeline](https://deepmind.google/science/alphafold/): July 2022 milestone and model background.
2. [AlphaFold Protein Structure Database](https://alphafold.ebi.ac.uk/): the public prediction resource.

## Limits

Prediction confidence varies. A predicted structure is not experimental confirmation of every feature, interaction, or biological function. The release count is not a count of experimentally solved proteins or approved treatments.

This repository checks the published milestone and resource, not the accuracy of every entry.
