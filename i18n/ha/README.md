**Sakamako na gaske da aka samu da taimakon AI. Tare da tushe.**

Tarin nasarorin AI da mutanen da suka samar da su. Kowane misali yana bayyana aikin AI, aikin mutane da iyakar hujjoji.

## Misalai

### Murya daga siginar ƙwaƙwalwa · 2025

Masu bincike sun haɗa na'urar da aka dasa a ƙwaƙwalwa da AI don samar da magana mai ci gaba ga wata mahalarta mai tsananin shanyewar jiki. Sun yi amfani da tsoffin rikodin muryarta kafin rauni. AI ya fassara siginar da ta shafi magana; mahalartar ta yi ƙoƙarin magana don horo, ƙungiyar kuma ta gina ta gwada tsarin. Wannan binciken asibiti ne mai buƙatar na'urar dasawa da horon mutum, ba dawo da aikin murya na halitta ba.

[Tushe](https://www.universityofcalifornia.edu/news/brain-voice-neuroprosthesis-restores-naturalistic-speech) · [Cikakken bayani da Turanci](../../cases/brain-to-voice.md)

### Rubutu a cikin nadadden takarda da ya ƙone · 2024

Ƙungiyar Vesuvius Challenge ta nuna sassan ginshiƙan rubutu 15 a cikin takardar Herculaneum da take nade. AI ya gano tawada a saman hotunan da aka buɗe a kwamfuta. Mutane sun ɗauki hotuna, shirya saman, gina samfura kuma karanta rubutun. Wannan dawo da wani sashe ne da masu shirya gasar suka sanar kuma suka duba, ba karanta ɗakin karatu gaba ɗaya ba.

[Tushe](https://scrollprize.org/grandprize) · [Cikakken bayani da Turanci](../../cases/herculaneum-scrolls.md)

### Fiye da hasashen tsarin furotin miliyan 200 · 2022

AlphaFold da EMBL-EBI sun buɗe fiye da hasashen tsarin furotin miliyan 200. AI ya yi hasashen; masu bincike sun gina kuma sun gwada samfurin, ƙungiyoyi sun wallafa bayanai, masana kuma suna tantance amfaninsu. Matakin tabbas ya bambanta. Hasashe ba tabbatarwar gwaji ga kowane tsari ba ne, kuma ba gano magunguna masu wannan yawa ba ne.

[Tushe](https://deepmind.google/science/alphafold/) · [Cikakken bayani da Turanci](../../cases/alphafold.md)

### Hanyoyin jera bayanai masu sauri · 2023

AlphaDev ya nemo jerin umarni masu inganci. Masu bincike sun mayar da hanyoyin da aka gano zuwa C++ kuma sun haɗa gyare-gyare cikin LLVM libc++. Mutane sun tsara aikin, duba sakamako kuma haɗa lambar. Amfanin sauri ya danganta da hanya da yanayin gwaji, ba dukkan shirye-shirye ba. Wannan aikin bai sake yin gwajin aiki ba.

[Tushe](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphadev-discovers-faster-sorting-algorithms/) · [Cikakken bayani da Turanci](../../cases/alphadev.md)

### Ƙarin ƙarfin lissafi da za a iya amfani da shi · 2025

Google ya ce dokar tsara ayyuka da aka samu ta AlphaEvolve ta dawo da matsakaicin 0.7% na albarkatun lissafinsa a duniya. AI ya ba da shawarar lamba kuma ya inganta ta da gwajin atomatik; injiniyoyi sun tsara matsala, tantancewa da amfani na zahiri. Adadin rahoton kamfanin ne, ba bincike mai zaman kansa ba. Dawo da ƙarfin aiki ba yana nufin rage wutar lantarki, kuɗi ko hayaƙi da kaso ɗaya ba.

[Tushe](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphaevolve-a-gemini-powered-coding-agent-for-designing-advanced-algorithms/) · [Cikakken bayani da Turanci](../../cases/alphaevolve.md)
