**Real things AI helped accomplish. Sources included.**

An open collection of outcomes made possible or improved by AI — and the people behind them. Each case explains what happened, what AI contributed, what humans contributed, and what the evidence supports.

## Start here

### 🗣️ A voice from brain signals · 2025

Researchers combined a brain implant with AI to synthesize speech for a participant with severe paralysis, using a voice personalized to resemble her own.

**AI's role:** Decoding speech-related brain activity into streaming audio. **Scope:** Clinical research using an implanted device and participant-specific training.

[Read the case →](cases/brain-to-voice.md) · [Paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-025-01905-6)

### 📜 Words inside a carbonized scroll · 2024

A Vesuvius Challenge team revealed 15 partial columns of writing inside a still-rolled Herculaneum scroll.

**AI's role:** Detecting ink in scanned, virtually unwrapped surfaces. **Human contribution:** Imaging, preparing the surfaces, developing the methods, and interpreting the text.

[Read the case →](cases/herculaneum-scrolls.md) · [Announcement](https://scrollprize.org/grandprize)

### 🧬 Over 200 million protein structure predictions · 2022

AlphaFold and EMBL-EBI released a freely accessible collection of over 200 million predicted protein structures.

**AI's role:** Predicting protein structures. **Scope:** Confidence varies; these are predictions, not individual experimental confirmations.

[Read the case →](cases/alphafold.md) · [Project](https://deepmind.google/science/alphafold/)

### ⚙️ Faster sorting routines in a widely used library · 2023

AlphaDev discovered sorting routines that researchers translated into C++ and incorporated into LLVM libc++.

**AI's role:** Searching for efficient instruction sequences. **Scope:** Performance gains depend on the sorting task and test conditions.

[Read the case →](cases/alphadev.md) · [Paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06004-9)

### 🖥️ More usable capacity in Google's data centers · 2025

Google reported that a scheduling heuristic discovered with AlphaEvolve recovered an average of 0.7% of its worldwide compute resources.

**AI's role:** Proposing and improving scheduling code through automated evaluation. **Evidence:** A deployment result reported by Google, not an independent audit.

[Read the case →](cases/alphaevolve.md) · [Technical account](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphaevolve-a-gemini-powered-coding-agent-for-designing-advanced-algorithms/)

## What belongs here?

A concrete outcome with an identifiable AI contribution and evidence a reader can inspect. Research breakthroughs, useful software, creative work, and everyday accomplishments are all welcome.

**The strength of a claim should match the strength of its evidence.** This collection includes both specialized AI systems and language-model-based tools. Each entry identifies its system and evidence type; inclusion does not mean this project has independently reproduced the result.

## Contribute a case

[Submit a case →](https://github.com/kaluli123123/ai-did-that/issues/new?template=submit-a-case.yml) Tell us what happened and when, what AI did, what people did, where to find evidence, and which limitations matter.

Firsthand accounts are welcome and will be labeled as such. Submissions in any supported language are welcome; translated editions include prefilled submission links in that language. See the [selection and contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [case template](templates/case-template.md).
